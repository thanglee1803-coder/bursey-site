Fair questions.

Do I have to post my card?

No. Posting is never required. Bursey works as a private scoreboard too.

I already track this in a spreadsheet.

Then keep it. Bursey isn't a CRM and doesn't store your contacts. It is one tap per try, a streak and a card. And with the paid plan your data exports as a CSV file.

It's a one-person app. Will it exist next year?

I can't promise the future. What I can say: your data lives on your device, the paid plan can export it, and I use Bursey myself every day.

Does counting tries get me clients?

No app can promise that. Bursey shows you how much you really did, and helps you keep doing it on the days nothing lands.

What about Mac and Windows?

iPhone comes first. Mac and Windows are planned as a download from this site, with no date yet. Tick the box below so I know you want it.
