2. The short version

  • This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising.

  • The only personal data involved is what any web server sees when you open a page (section 3), and the email you send if you join the waitlist (section 5).

3. Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers, including your IP address, the time, the page you asked for and your browser type. GitHub needs this data to deliver the site and to protect it against attacks, and may keep it in server logs for a short time.

Legal basis: my legitimate interest in showing this website safely and reliably (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). The data may be processed in the USA. GitHub takes part in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has decided that the level of data protection is adequate (Art. 45 GDPR). More in GitHub's privacy statement.

4. The sound setting in your browser

If you switch the page's sound off, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage on your own device. It is not sent to me or anyone else, and you can remove it by clearing your browser's site data. Storing it is needed for the setting you asked for (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

5. The waitlist

There is no database behind this page. When you press "Join the waitlist", your own email program opens with a prepared message to me. Only when you send it do I receive your email address, anything you write, and the boxes you ticked (beta, Mac, Windows).

I use this only to tell you when Bursey is available and, if you ticked it, to invite you to the beta. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with a short email to anhjuniorle@gmail.com; then I delete your address. Otherwise I delete waitlist emails at the latest when the waitlist is closed.

My email account is provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, which stores the emails for me. Google may also process data in the USA and takes part in the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

6. Contact by email or phone

If you write or call me, I use your details only to answer you (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (f) GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires me to keep them.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data (Art. 15 GDPR), to have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive it in a portable format (Art. 20), and to object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21). To use any of these rights, email anhjuniorle@gmail.com.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for me: Die Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit der Freien Hansestadt Bremen.

8. Changes

When the site changes, for example when bursey.app goes online or the waitlist moves to a real sign-up form, I update this page.
